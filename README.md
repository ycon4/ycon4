## <img src="https://api.iconify.design/lucide:user.svg?color=%2358a6ff" width="24" height="24" align="absmiddle" alt="" /> About Me

Hey, I'm **Andrei**, but most people call me **Ycon**. I like building things with data and code. Most days that means training ML models, putting together dashboards that are actually easy to read, and building web apps.

My favorite projects are the ones that start as a messy dataset or a rough idea and end up as something people can use. I also have a design side, so I care about how things look and feel, not just whether they run.
